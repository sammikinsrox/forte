# Credits

Forte's piano is a physical model; no recordings ship with it. Its voicing was fitted to
measurements of these references, taken locally (SPEC §3.1). Only derived numbers are in this
repository (`data/reference-measurements/`), never audio.

- **University of Iowa Electronic Music Studios, Musical Instrument Samples** (Steinway, pp/mf/ff):
  the first hammer, felt and decay fits (issue 0021).
- **The user's own renders of Spectrasonics Keyscape** (dry note sweeps and pieces): the voicing
  target since 2026-11-16 (issue 0021 §14 on, issue 0022). Keyscape is a trademark of
  Spectrasonics; Forte is not affiliated with or endorsed by Spectrasonics.

Planned for calibration, not yet used: Salamander Grand Piano (CC BY), Piano in 162, and the Plath
concert-grand dataset (CC BY 4.0).

Software and fonts: see `THIRD-PARTY.md`.
