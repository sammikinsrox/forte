Forte, first notes

Each row plays a real Steinway note (University of Iowa recordings) and Forte's physical model of the same note, in sync. Switching between them keeps your place, so you hear the difference at the same moment of the note.

Forte's levels are matched to the recording at loud (ff) only. Soft and medium notes come from slower hammer speeds, so their loudness and brightness come from the physics. Headphones help.
